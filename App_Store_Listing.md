# App Store Listing Information

## App Name *
Apex

## Short Description *
A comprehensive business management app for tracking products, managing sales, and controlling inventory in small retail businesses.

## Full Description *
Apex is a powerful mobile application designed specifically for small retail businesses to streamline their operations and boost productivity. Whether you're an owner managing multiple staff members or a sales associate handling daily transactions, Apex provides all the tools you need in one intuitive interface.

### Key Features:
- **Product Management**: Add, edit, and track your products with detailed information including pricing, stock levels, and categories
- **Inventory Control**: Monitor stock transactions (stock in, stock out, damage, returns) with real-time updates
- **Sales Management**: Create and track sales transactions, generate receipts, and manage customer interactions
- **Supplier Management**: Maintain supplier information and relationships for efficient procurement
- **Staff Management**: For business owners, manage multiple staff members with role-based access control
- **Expense Tracking**: Record and categorize business expenses for better financial oversight
- **Comprehensive Reports**: Generate daily reports, profit reports, and business analytics
- **Multi-language Support**: Available in English and Swahili for broader accessibility

### User Roles:
- **Owner**: Full access to all features including staff management, reports, and system administration
- **Staff/Seller**: Access to product browsing, sales creation, stock management, and basic reporting
