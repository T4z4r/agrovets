# App Store Listing Information

## App Name *
Apex

## Short Description *
A comprehensive business management app for tracking products, managing sales, and controlling inventory in agrovets businesses.

## Full Description *
Apex is a powerful mobile application designed specifically for agricultural supply businesses (agrovets) to streamline their operations and boost productivity. Whether you're an owner managing multiple sellers or a seller handling daily transactions, Apex provides all the tools you need in one intuitive interface.

### Key Features:
- **Product Management**: Add, edit, and track your agricultural products with detailed information including pricing, stock levels, and categories
- **Inventory Control**: Monitor stock transactions (stock in, stock out, damage, returns) with real-time updates
- **Sales Management**: Create and track sales transactions, generate receipts, and manage customer interactions
- **Supplier Management**: Maintain supplier information and relationships for efficient procurement
- **Seller Management**: For business owners, manage multiple sellers with role-based access control
- **Expense Tracking**: Record and categorize business expenses for better financial oversight
- **Comprehensive Reports**: Generate daily reports, profit reports, and business analytics
- **Multi-language Support**: Available in English and Swahili for broader accessibility

### User Roles:
- **Owner**: Full access to all features including seller management, reports, and system administration
- **Seller**: Access to product browsing, sales creation, stock management, and basic reporting

- Agricultural supply stores
- Veterinary clinics
- Farm equipment dealers
- Livestock feed suppliers
- Agricultural cooperatives

Apex helps agrovets businesses digitize their operations, reduce manual errors, and make data-driven decisions to grow their business.
