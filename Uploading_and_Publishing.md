# Uploading and Publishing Apex App

## 1. Upload App Bundle

1. Go to "Production" > "Create new release"
2. Upload the `app-release.aab` file
3. Fill release notes: "Initial release of Apex - Agricultural management app with product tracking, sales management, and inventory control."
4. Review and confirm

## 2. Set Pricing and Distribution

### Pricing
- Set to Free or specify price if monetizing

### Countries
- Select target countries (start with your primary market, expand later)

### Content rating
- Confirm the rating from the questionnaire

## 3. Publish the App

1. Review all information in the checklist
2. Click "Start rollout to production"
3. Choose rollout percentage (start with 20% for testing, then 100%)
4. Wait for review (usually 1-3 days)

## 4. Monitor and Update

- Check for any review feedback or issues
- Monitor crash reports and user feedback
- Plan for future updates with new features or bug fixes

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **AAB Upload Errors**: Ensure the file is not corrupted and signing is correct
- **Policy Violations**: Double-check privacy policy and content rating
- **App Not Live**: Wait for indexing (up to 24 hours after approval)
- **Rejections**: Address specific feedback from Google reviewers
