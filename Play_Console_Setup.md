# Google Play Console Setup for Apex App

## 1. Create a New App

1. Log in to Google Play Console
2. Click "Create app"
3. Fill in:
   - App name: Apex
   - Default language: English (en-US)
   - App type: App (not game)
   - Free or paid: Free (or Paid if applicable)

## 2. App Details

### Main store listing
- Short description (80 characters max): "Comprehensive agricultural management app for tracking products, sales, and inventory."
- Full description (4000 characters max): Describe features like product management, sales tracking, stock control, expense management, reporting, and barcode scanning.
- Screenshots (upload 2-8 per device type): Include screens for dashboard, product list, sales form, stock management, reports.
- Icon (512x512 PNG): Use the prepared app icon
- Feature graphic (1024x500 PNG): Showcase the app's agricultural focus

### Categorization
- Category: Business or Productivity
- Tags: agriculture, inventory, sales, management

### Contact details
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)

### Privacy policy
- Enter the URL of your privacy policy

## 3. Content Rating

Complete the content rating questionnaire. For an agricultural management app:
- No mature content
- Select appropriate ratings for any user-generated content if applicable

## 4. Target Audience

- Specify if the app is directed at children under 13 (likely not for this business app)
